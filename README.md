> A full-stack local marketplace that helps roadside and local street vendors build an online presence and connect with nearby customers.

## 📌 Overview

Street Connect is a full-stack web application designed to digitally connect local street vendors with nearby customers.

Many roadside vendors depend mainly on physical foot traffic and have limited online presence. Street Connect gives these vendors a simple digital platform to showcase their shop and products, manage orders, and share their location.

Customers can discover nearby vendors, browse their products, add items to a cart, place orders, and track order status.

The platform includes separate experiences for **Customers, Vendors, and Admins**.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Many local roadside vendors:

- Have little or no online presence
- Are difficult for nearby customers to discover
- Do not have a simple way to showcase their products online
- May have limited experience using complex digital platforms

At the same time, customers may not know what local vendors are available around them.

## 💡 Solution

Street Connect provides a simple digital bridge between local vendors and nearby customers.

Vendors can create their digital shop, upload shop and product images, manage products, share their location, and handle customer orders.

Customers can discover vendors, browse products, place orders, and track their purchases.

---

# ✨ Features

## 👤 Customer

- Customer registration and login
- Discover local vendors
- View vendor profiles
- View vendor products
- View product details
- Add products to cart
- Checkout
- Place orders
- View order history
- Track order status
- Receive notifications
- Responsive mobile-friendly interface

## 🧑‍🌾 Vendor

- Vendor registration and login
- Create and manage shop profile
- Upload shop image
- Add products
- Edit products
- Delete products
- Upload product images
- Manage product stock
- Share current shop location
- View customer orders
- Accept or reject orders
- Update order status
- View notifications
- Mobile-friendly vendor interface

The vendor experience is intentionally designed to be simple and visual for roadside vendors who may have limited experience with digital applications.

## 🛠️ Admin

- Admin dashboard
- Marketplace management capabilities
- Vendor/order management features supported by the current application
